Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you use our Discord bot.

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Montreve Group ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Montreve Group Discord bot and how that information may be used, stored, and protected.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and how the bot is configured, the Service may process the following information:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, and channel IDs.
  • Server settings, bot configuration, and role information.
  • Commands and information submitted when using bot features.
  • Moderation records or administrative logs, where enabled.
  • Technical error information needed to diagnose problems.

We aim to process only information reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, and protect the Service. The exact information processed depends on the features actually in use.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the bot's features.
  • Maintain server configurations and administrative functions.
  • Support moderation, security, and abuse prevention.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and legitimate inquiries.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored for as long as necessary to provide the relevant features, maintain security, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the bot's configuration. We do not promise that all records are automatically deleted at a particular time unless that functionality is explicitly provided.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the bot, including Discord and the bot's hosting or database providers, where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, address security incidents, enforce our Terms of Service, or protect users and the Service.

5. Discord and Third-Party Services

The bot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policy. We do not control Discord's independent data practices or those of other third-party services.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information handled by the Service. However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may stop interacting with the bot at any time. Depending on the information involved and applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with you.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal reasons. We may need enough information to verify and respond to a request.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended to bypass Discord's minimum age requirements or applicable laws concerning children's privacy. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service or our practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the Montreve Group administration through the official support channel associated with the bot.
